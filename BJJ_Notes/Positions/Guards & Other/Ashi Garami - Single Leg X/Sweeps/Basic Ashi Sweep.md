### Description
By lifting your hips up and to the side, turning your opponent's knee while also blocking the far leg with your opposite foot behind their hamstring you can create an off balance and sweep.

### Key Details
- Use the heel of your foot on the hip to bring your hips off the ground close to your opponent's leg
- your secondary leg's shins should be parallel with your primary leg
- Flex the foot of your secondary leg
**Hand position**
- Primary hand controlling the leg can wrap around the leg for better position when going for a straight ankle lock after sweeping
- or for a better chance of off balancing you can wrap the ankle and bring your hand behind your back
- Secondary hand for better control keeping your primary leg on your opponent's hip you can hold your shin
- or for really easy off balancing you can grab the other ankle/pants (if you can reach it)
- another option is you can push off the ground beside your head for some extra pressure.
