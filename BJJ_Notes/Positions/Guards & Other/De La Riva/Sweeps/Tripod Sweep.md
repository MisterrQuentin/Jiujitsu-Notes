### Description
Push your opponents hip with one foot while pulling their ankle and the other foot that's behind their knee

### Key Details
- your hand that's grabbing the ankle should be touching the floor, the higher it is, the less leverage you have.
- Your hip should be next to or on top of your opponents foot to prevent them from kicking it out and breaking the ankle grip.
- the foot behind the knee is just preventing your opponent from stepping back.
- 

### Defense
[[Tripod Sweep Defense]]
- By grabbing the sleeve that's not grabbing the ankle and pulling, you can prevent the sweep
- push down the leg trying to hook your knee and step back
- you can sometimes shift your hips away so the foot on the hip falls off
